All-Inclusive Hotel vs. Owning/Renting Your Own Apartment in Hurghada: Freedom, Economy, and a Different Quality of Life

Useful Information • Hurghada • ≈ 8 min read

A package tour to Hurghada on an "all-inclusive" basis has been the most familiar way to vacation on the Red Sea for decades. However, an increasing number of travelers and freelancers are choosing an alternative path — long-term rental or purchasing their own apartment in Egypt.

Let’s delve into why renting an apartment in Hurghada or owning your own property offers a fundamentally different level of comfort and freedom compared to buying a hotel package.

1. Freedom from Hotel Constraints and a Different Level of Comfort

The biggest drawback of a hotel vacation is the rigid attachment to a schedule: breakfasts by the hour, lines at restaurants, and limited grounds. If you decide to rent an apartment in Hurghada for a month or long-term, your schedule belongs only to you.

  • Space and Autonomy: Instead of a standard 20-meter room, you get a full-fledged home with a kitchen, living room, and balcony.
  • Privacy: No noise from animation and crowds of tourists by the pool.
  • Beach Choice: Staying in a hotel complex limits you to one shoreline. Owners and renters of apartments can choose new locations every day — from cozy private zones in El Gouna to wide sandy beaches in the Al Kawthar area.

2. Financial Benefit: The Economics of Long-Term Stays

If your goal is to spend from two weeks to several months at the sea, a package tour becomes catastrophically unprofitable.

  • Long-term Rental: Calculate how much it costs to rent an apartment in Hurghada for a month: the price of quality housing with renovations starts from $200–400 per month. At the same time, a 2-week package for two in a good four or five-star hotel will cost from $1500–2000.
  • Flexibility of Dates: You can rent an apartment in Hurghada by the day for a short vacation or arrange for renting an apartment in Hurghada long-term (for six months to a year), reducing the monthly payment to a minimum.
  • Meals and Daily Life: Buying fresh seafood, mangoes, and vegetables at local markets costs many times less than hotel meals, and the quality of products is often significantly higher.

3. Location and Infrastructure: Russian Neighborhoods and Premium Residences

Hotels are often isolated from city life. By renting or buying real estate, you choose your surroundings:

  • Russian-speaking Neighborhoods: The idea of buying an apartment in Hurghada in the Russian neighborhood (e.g., Al Kawthar or Intercontinental) is extremely popular. Here, all infrastructure is adapted: within walking distance are Russian kindergartens, pharmacies, shops with familiar products, and Russian-speaking services.
  • Premium Segment: For those who appreciate maximum level, an excellent solution is the desire to buy an apartment in Hurghada (Esplanada) or in similar elite residential complexes on the first line with their own promenade and private beach.

4. Buying Real Estate in Egypt: Personal Home by the Sea and Investments

When frequent trips become a lifestyle, the question arises of acquiring your own housing. The request apartment in Hurghada to buy by the sea inexpensively is one of the most frequent among citizens of Europe and international buyers.

Real Cost and Resale Market

Market dynamics show that the cost of an apartment in Hurghada remains one of the most affordable among the world's seaside resorts. A small studio or apartment in Hurghada with 1 bedroom can be purchased for prices from $20,000 – $35,000. If considering buying an apartment in Hurghada (Egypt) resale, you can often find options with ready-made furniture and appliances "turnkey," requiring no additional investment.

Legal Side and Registration for Foreigners

Many are concerned with the question: how to buy an apartment in Hurghada for a foreigner and how to legally buy an apartment in Hurghada without risk?

  1. Rights of Foreigners: Citizens of foreign countries have the full right to buy real estate in Egypt in private ownership.
  2. Procedure: The deal is finalized by signing a purchase and sale agreement (Tawkil) and subsequent registration of ownership rights in court (Saha Wa Tanfeez) or with the registration authority (Shahr El Akar).
  3. Safety: The key is to conduct the transaction through proven agencies and an independent Egyptian lawyer who will check the seller’s documents for any encumbrances.

5. Comparison of Key Options: Package Hotel vs Renting or Owning an Apartment

To clearly see the difference, let’s compare both formats across the most important aspects of daily life:

  • Living Costs: At a hotel, you pay per day per person, which quickly accumulates into a massive bill during stays of a month or longer. With an apartment (rented or owned), you pay a fixed monthly rate or just utilities, saving up to 70% of your budget during longer stays.
  • Freedom and Routine: Hotels enforce strict schedules, from fixed dining hours to housekeeping visits. Having your own place offers 100% independence: you decide when to wake up, eat, work, or host guests.
  • Food Quality: Hotel buffets can grow repetitive and lack local authenticity. With a kitchen of your own, you can prepare fresh seafood and tropical fruits from local markets or explore diverse city restaurants whenever you choose.
  • Living Space: A standard hotel room typically offers 20–30 square meters of single-room space. An apartment provides a complete home layout with a dedicated bedroom, fully equipped kitchen, living area, workspace, and balcony.
  • Financial Value: Paying for hotel packages represents an unrecoverable vacation expense. Purchasing an apartment retains your financial asset, offering potential equity appreciation and passive rental income when you are away.

How to Look for Options?

If you are planning to rent an apartment in Hurghada inexpensively for a month or are looking for buying options:

  • From the Owner: You can rent an apartment from the owner in Hurghada through specialized groups on social networks and local communities — this will save on the realtor commission.
  • Platforms: Many search for options through international real estate services where objects from direct owners are listed.
  • Long-term Rental on Site: If you need to rent an apartment in Hurghada long-term or rent an apartment in Hurghada Egypt for a month, it is optimal to come for 2-3 days to a hotel and look at options live together with a realtor.

Summary

A package hotel is ideal for a short 7-day reboot vacation. But if you want to live by the sea for months, work remotely in comfort, or invest money in a liquid asset, apartment sales in Hurghada and the long-term rental market offer options for every budget. Owning or renting an apartment in Hurghada turns banal Egypt from an "all-inclusive" destination into your second home on the Red Sea coast.
