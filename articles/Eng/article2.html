Complete Guide to the Internet in Hurghada: Mobile Networks, Providers, and Broadband

Useful information • Hurghada • ≈ 6 min read

Hurghada is a popular Red Sea resort where reliable internet and good connectivity are essential for remote work, leisure, and daily life. In this article, we will detail how to choose a mobile operator, where to buy a SIM card, how home broadband works, and key nuances to keep in mind when renting or buying real estate.

Mobile Operators in Egypt

Four main telecom companies operate in the Egyptian mobile market. While each offers strong plans, network coverage can vary significantly depending on the specific district in Hurghada — whether in lively Sakala, touristy Mamsha, quiet El Ahyaa, or upscale Sahl Hasheesh.

Vodafone Egypt — One of the most popular operators, providing wide coverage and high mobile internet speeds across most areas of the city.

Etisalat Egypt — Offers excellent data plans and fast connection speeds, working reliably in central locations.

Orange — A solid operator offering budget-friendly mobile data packages and convenient tourist offers.

WE (Telecom Egypt) — The national operator, actively expanding its mobile network while maintaining total dominance in fixed-line broadband.

Essential Tip When Renting or Buying Property

Mobile signal quality and speed can vary greatly depending on the district and the specific building — due to wall thickness, floor level, or window orientation. In some compounds, one operator might deliver maximum speeds, while another barely catches a signal indoors. Always test the mobile coverage of your preferred operator on-site before signing a lease or purchasing property.

Where to Buy a SIM Card?

The easiest and quickest way to get connected upon arrival is to buy a SIM card directly at Hurghada Airport upon exiting the baggage claim area. Official kiosks for all major operators are located right there. You will need your passport to complete the registration.

Home Broadband (Home DSL): The Main Player

The undisputed leader in fixed-line home internet in Egypt is WE. Almost all broadband infrastructure throughout the country relies on their network.

How to Tell If a Building Is Connected to WE?

Visual Inspection: Many buildings in Hurghada are already connected to WE internet. To check, inspect the facade or entrance area — typically, a metal or plastic box featuring the WE logo is mounted on or near the building if it is connected.

Checking with the Owner: When choosing an apartment, always confirm the internet connectivity status of the building with the landlord or management company.

What If the Building Is Connected, But the Apartment Is Not?

If a WE distribution box is on the building but the cabling hasn't been run into the apartment, you need to visit an official WE office. Their technicians will process your application, route the cable into your unit, and complete the setup. Please note that the router must be purchased separately at the WE store.

What If the Building Is Not Connected At All?

If the building lacks WE infrastructure entirely, you can submit a request at a WE office to connect the whole property. However, keep in mind that this requires additional approvals and time, making it significantly more complex and lengthy than activating an apartment in an already connected building.

Official Provider Websites

To check current rate plans, terms, and promotional offers, visit the official websites: Vodafone Egypt, Etisalat Egypt, Orange Egypt, WE (Telecom Egypt).

Important Notice for Imported Phones (IMEI Tax)

If you brought a smartphone from abroad (i.e., not purchased officially within Egypt), pay close attention to local telecommunication regulations.

The 3-Month Rule: According to the regulations of the National Telecom Regulatory Authority (NTRA), imported devices can operate on local SIM cards without paying duty for 90 days (3 months). After this period, unregistered devices may be blocked from local networks — showing No Service.

Registration & Customs Tax: To keep the phone operational on local SIMs, the required state import duty must be paid.

The Telephony App: Once the tax is paid, your phone's IMEI must appear in the official government registry. You can check your registration status and manage device compliance using the official Telephony app (developed by NTRA), available in app stores — Google Play Store for Android and App Store for iOS — or by consulting NTRA support and local operator resources.
