Living in Hurghada Outside Hotels: Complete Guide to Daily Life, Transport, and Healthcare

Useful Information • Hurghada • ≈ 10 min read

Living as an independent resident or long-term renter in Hurghada is fundamentally different from a standard all-inclusive hotel stay. Stepping outside resort walls unlocks a comfortable, budget-friendly coastal lifestyle.

1. Shopping Malls, Cinemas, and Duty Free

Modern shopping malls serve as primary hubs for grocery shopping and entertainment. The main center of attraction is **Senzo Mall**, the premier shopping center in Hurghada. It houses the massive *Spinneys* hypermarket, offering imported foods, local produce, and electronics. Additionally, **Senzo Mall** features a top-quality cinema showing major movie releases in original languages with subtitles.

In the northern part of town, **Hurghada City Center** features a huge Carrefour and an indoor aquarium. For tax-free purchases, **Duty Free Hurghada** allows international travelers to buy items within 48 hours of arrival upon presenting a passport.

2. Local Transport and Getting Around Egypt

Navigating the city is easy when utilizing modern transport options:

  • Taxis: Traditional orange taxis frequently overcharge tourists. It is much safer to rely on ride-hailing apps like Uber or InDrive for fixed, transparent fares.
  • Vehicle Rentals: You can easily rent a car or scooter for complete independence around town.
  • Intercity Travel: Comfortable long-distance buses like Go Bus offer affordable, direct transport to Cairo, Luxor, and Alexandria.
  • Airport Transfer: Arranging a dedicated airport transfer in Hurghada via apps or local agencies is ideal when arriving with heavy luggage.

3. Healthcare, Pharmacies, and El Kawsar Hospitals

Egyptian pharmaceutical prices are strictly regulated by the government. Any pharmacy in Hurghada provides genuine European medications and high-quality local equivalents at a fraction of Western prices.

Medical tourism, particularly affordable dental care (implants and prosthetics), is rapidly growing in Hurghada. Always choose doctors based on direct expat recommendations.

Addresses of Two Recommended Hospitals in El Kawsar:

  • The Egyptian Hospital: Ahmed Saied Street, El Kawther Area, Hurghada. (Modern multi-specialty facility with 24/7 emergency service).
  • Al Kawther Hospital: Building 37, Airport Road / Banks Street (behind Barclays/Attijariwafa Bank), El Kawther, Hurghada. (Established medical center providing high-standard patient care).

4. Delivery Services, Fast Food, and Home Visa Service

Daily domestic conveniences are readily available:

  • Grocery & Meal Delivery: Use the Talabat mobile app to deliver ready meals, 19L bottled water, and groceries straight from Spinneys.
  • Fast Food Chains: International chains such as McDonald's, KFC, and Pizza Hut are located along the main promenades and malls.
  • Visa Renewal at Home: Licensed visa service agencies handle tourist and residence visa extensions, delivering your completed residency card directly to your doorstep without needing to visit the Gawazat immigration office.

5. Currency Exchange and EGP Exchange Rate

Keep track of the official exchange rate of the Egyptian pound through banking apps. You can exchange foreign currencies safely at 24/7 bank ATM Exchange Machines (Change Machines) located near major banks and shopping malls.

6. Emergency Numbers

Save these essential emergency contacts on your phone:

  • Universal Emergency: 112
  • Tourist Police: 126
  • Police: 122
  • Ambulance: 123
  • Fire Department: 180
