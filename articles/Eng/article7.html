Why Wintering and Vacationing in Hurghada Is Now Better Value Than Thailand, Bali, and Mexico: Relocation and Rental Guide

Useful Info • Hurghada • ≈ 5 min read

Over the past couple of years, traveling to popular long-haul resorts has turned into a serious test for any budget. Flights from Europe, Serbia, and Russia to Southeast Asia (Thailand, Bali, Vietnam), Latin America (Mexico, the Dominican Republic, Costa Rica), and Indian Ocean islands (Maldives, Zanzibar) have become noticeably more expensive. High airfares are now compounded by exhausting layovers and rising accommodation and dining costs at the destinations themselves.

Against this backdrop, Egypt has become the most practical and affordable warm-sea destination. The flight takes just 4–5 hours from most European capitals, Belgrade, or Moscow. Direct scheduled and charter flights are plentiful, and airfare costs a fraction of flights to Asia or the Americas.

Yet the real secret to getting maximum value in Hurghada is skipping package hotels in favor of renting your own apartment.

Why Renting an Apartment in Hurghada Is Cheaper and More Comfortable Than a Hotel

Independent apartment rentals (for a month, a season, or a couple of weeks) transform your travel experience and save up to 40–60% of your budget compared to all-inclusive hotels.

  • Genuine savings on housing and food. Renting a good studio or one-bedroom apartment for a full month costs less than a single week at a decent hotel. Meanwhile, groceries in Hurghada are inexpensive: fresh vegetables, fruit, seafood, and meat at local markets and supermarkets (such as Carrefour or Gomla) let you eat well and enjoy plenty of variety on a modest budget.
  • Freedom from hotel schedules and crowds. You are not tied to buffet hours, you do not have round-the-clock animation noise beneath your windows, and you never have to compete for sun loungers by the pool.
  • Comfort for remote work and families. An apartment comes with a full kitchen, a washing machine, a separate bedroom, and a proper workspace—ideal for remote workers, families with children, and long-stay winter visitors.
  • Access to resort-style amenities. Most modern residential complexes (compounds) in Hurghada feature gated grounds with swimming pools, 24/7 security, gyms, and even private beaches.

Hurghada Neighborhoods: Housing for Every Taste and Budget

Hurghada stretches nearly 40 kilometers along the coast, and each district offers its own lifestyle and price segment:

  1. El Kawther — the most popular neighborhood among Europeans and international expats. All key urban infrastructure is concentrated here: supermarkets, coffee shops, fitness clubs, European bakeries, and the Mamsha pedestrian promenade. Options range from budget-friendly studios in city buildings to spacious apartments in gated compounds with swimming pools.
  2. El Ahyaa — a northern district actively developing with modern resort residences. Ideal for those seeking peace and quiet, brand-new sea-view apartments at very accessible prices, and proximity to kitesurfing stations.
  3. Sahl Hasheesh and Makadi Bay — upscale gated resort communities south of Hurghada. The top choice for maximum aesthetic comfort, featuring a serene beachfront promenade free of city noise, golf courses, designer apartments, and lagoon-side villas.
  4. El Gouna — the "Venice of Egypt" just north of Hurghada. An exclusive private lagoon town with its own yacht marina, fine dining, coworking spaces, and distinctive atmosphere. Prices here are higher than in Hurghada proper, while the service standards and community rival top European resorts.
  5. Hadaba and Sheraton — the historic heart of the city. A great option for living on a tight budget within walking distance of traditional markets, seafood restaurants, Hurghada Marina, and numerous public and hotel beaches with coral reefs.

The Greatest Asset — The Red Sea and Active Water Sports

Beyond financial value, Hurghada outperforms most global beach destinations in water quality alone. Unlike many beaches in Asia or the Americas with murky water, heavy surf, and extreme tides, the Red Sea in Hurghada stays crystal clear, warm, and calm year-round.

  • World-class diving and snorkeling. The underwater ecosystem here is among the richest on the planet. House coral reefs teeming with sea turtles, rays, and hundreds of colorful fish species are accessible right from the shore at select beaches or via a short boat trip to Giftun Island and Orange Bay.
  • A mecca for kitesurfing and windsurfing. Thanks to steady winds and vast shallow lagoons (especially in El Ahyaa and El Gouna), Hurghada ranks among the world's top spots for learning and riding. Dozens of international schools with certified instructors operate along the coast.

Relocating to Hurghada for a few weeks or months in a rented apartment offers a rare combination today: European everyday convenience, a short flight, substantial budget savings, and direct access to one of the finest seas in the world.
